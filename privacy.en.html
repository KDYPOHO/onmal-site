Onmal Privacy Notice

For people outside Korea · The Korean privacy policy is authoritative

This is the English notice for people outside Korea. The Korean privacy policy is the governing text; if the two differ, the Korean text prevails, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.

In short

  • Onmal processes sound and subtitles on your own PC. Microphone audio, game or chat audio, subtitles, settings and logs stay on your computer. They are not sent to the developer, and the developer cannot see them.
  • The only personal data the developer receives is an email you choose to send (support questions, bug reports, privacy requests).
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no automatic crash reports. We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not use it for profiling or advertising.
  • Subtitles and translations are produced automatically by AI models running on your PC. They can be wrong. Do not rely on them for important decisions.

1. Who is responsible

Onmal is made by an individual developer in the Republic of Korea, 강동엽 (Kang Dongyeop, "todak"), who is the controller and privacy contact for the data described here. Contact: todaklife@gmail.com (English or Korean).

2. What happens on your PC (not received by the developer)

DataWhat Onmal doesStored?
Microphone / game / chat audioConverted to text by a local AI modelNo recordings. Audio is held in memory only while being processed
Subtitles and translationsShown on screenNot saved to files. Voice-room subtitles keep the latest 100 lines in memory
Names of teammates (name hints)Used to spell names correctlyMemory only, cleared when Onmal closes
Settings, consent record, logsSaved in your Windows user folderYes, on your PC. Logs replace your user folder name with a placeholder

Voice room (optional). If you open a voice room with Steam friends, your voice is sent through Valve's Steam network only to the people in that room, and only after you agree and press push-to-talk. Valve's privacy policy applies to the Steam network. Only use it in conversations you are part of, and follow the recording/interception laws of your country.

Model downloads. Onmal may download AI model files over HTTPS. Like any download, this reveals your IP address to the download host. No audio or text is sent.

4. International transfer

Your email is stored by Google in the United States and other countries where Google operates. For people in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (check Google's current terms). If you do not want your data transferred, please do not email us; this does not affect your use of the app.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your email data, to receive a copy (portability), and to withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Email todaklife@gmail.com from the address you used. We answer within 10 days where possible and always within the period your law allows (GDPR: one month).

You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example:

  • EU/EEA: your national supervisory authority (list: edpb.europa.eu)
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)
  • Korea: Personal Information Protection Commission (privacy.go.kr)
  • Japan: Personal Information Protection Commission (ppc.go.jp)
  • Brazil: ANPD (gov.br/anpd)

US residents. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information. Onmal falls below the thresholds of the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws, but we honor access and deletion requests from anyone.

6. Children

Onmal is not directed at children. If you are under 13 (or under the age set by your country, e.g. 14 in Korea or 16 in some EU countries), please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If we learn we have, we delete it.

7. Security

The mailbox is used only by the developer, protected by two-factor authentication, and opened only on the developer's own up-to-date devices. Inside the app: model files are verified with SHA-256 before use, the local translation server listens only on 127.0.0.1 with a random key, and voice-room packets from non-members are rejected. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities within the time the law requires (72 hours where applicable).

8. AI transparency

Subtitles, translations and any synthesized speech are generated automatically by AI models on your PC and are labeled as AI output in the app. They may contain errors, especially names, slang and noisy audio.

9. Changes

When this notice changes in a way that matters, we announce it in the Steam news for Onmal and ship the new text with the app. Changes to the terms of use that need your agreement are shown in the app before voice features are used.

Effective: from the Onmal Playtest build that includes this file (November 2026).

10. This website

The official website uses no cookies, no analytics or advertising tools, and makes no requests for external fonts or scripts. It stores only your chosen language and color theme in your browser (onmal-lang and onmal-theme in localStorage); these values are never sent to the developer, and you can delete them by clearing site data.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., which may process access logs such as IP addresses to run and secure the service under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive these logs. Links to Steam, Discord and other services take you to their own policies.

Onmal is free software and makes no money from your data.
